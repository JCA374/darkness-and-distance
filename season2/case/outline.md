# Outline: *Darkness and Distance*, season two: *A Double Existence*

Trailer plus eight episodes, about 2,300 words each. Told in the order of Victor's life. Each episode: cold open →
recap/hook → the place and the find → the event, through the documents → the belief beat (for the theory) → the nag
(against it) → cliffhanger. Evidence IDs: season one `case/evidence.md`, season two `season2/case/evidence.md`.
Rules: season one 1–24, season two S1–S10 (`season2/case/bible.md`).

## ep00 — Trailer: The Only One There (about 350 words)
- Cold open: Walton, "Such a man has a double existence" (E101).
- Season one, she believed. Kessler's letter: "as if the man on the ice was the only one there".
- Stakes: the same dead (no bride, no cabin). The three doors: Victor alone; Victor with help; something else.
- "Three moments the theory can't explain. I'll give you every one."
- Close: "Could it really be him?"

## ep01 — A Double Existence (2,300)
- Cold open: Walton on Victor's eyes, "wildness, and even madness" (E100).
- Kessler's letter; the call; "I don't diagnose the dead"; split personality named and corrected (S2).
- The map, for new listeners: Geneva, Ingolstadt, the November night, two quiet years, Geneva, a courtroom, a glacier,
  Orkney, Ireland, home for a wedding, the ice. No outcomes.
- Kent: Judith, the box, the conservator (E88): the August 5th letter (the sighting) is one hand, one ink. Walton's portrait of Victor;
  Walton's line that Victor corrected the notes (E61, plant T1, fact only). "Remember, I am not recording the vision
  of a madman" (E102).
- Belief beat: Walton's "double existence" (E101), and what Kessler says it is and isn't.
- Nag: the crew saw a giant before Victor came aboard. "That's the first page. Keep it."
- Cliffhanger: Ingolstadt, a November night, and the first time Victor saw something nobody else did.
- Loops opened: L1 the sledge (closes ep08), L2 the corrections (ep05).

## ep02 — The Fever (2,300)
- Cold open: "I thought I saw the dreaded spectre glide into the room" (E104).
- Munich: the university archive; E80 recalled; the find E89 (ill of a fever, a winter).
- Ingolstadt: Waldman, two years' work, vaults (E09); the November night (E09a, E103): "He might have spoken, but I
  did not hear". The courtyard all night.
- Morning: Clerval; the laughter; the spectre; the fit; months of raving; Henry hides it from the family (E104).
- Kessler: what the page describes; why the theory starts here; S2 line on violence and illness.
- Belief beat: the only description of the creature's first night comes from a man about to fall into a fever.
- Nag: the town minute (E81): vaults robbed, bones missing. Something was taken. Okafor: why.
- Cliffhanger: nearly two years of quiet. Then a letter from his father.

## ep03 — My Own Spirit (2,300)
- Cold open: "nearly in the light of my own vampire, my own spirit let loose from the grave" (E105).
- William's life (Elizabeth's letter), the walk, the miniature, "the print of the murder's finger" (E10, E12).
- Where was Victor? Walking tour (E09c). The dead end (E90): no paper for a walk.
- The storm, the figure, the Salêve (E13). Kessler on "my own spirit".
- Okafor: a small child, any adult hand.
- Belief beat: Victor himself says the fever "would give an air of delirium" (E105, E14).
- Nag: Clerval. By Victor's account Henry was with him for that fortnight and stayed his friend for years.
- Cliffhanger: the miniature turns up in the pocket of someone in the house.

## ep04 — The Ravings of a Madman (2,300)
- Cold open: "A thousand times rather would I have confessed myself guilty…" (E106).
- Justine as a person (E11). The trial (E16–E21), Reber; the clerk's line read again (E25a).
- Victor in court, silent; "Thus I might proclaim myself a madman" (E107, plant T3).
- The prison: the find E91, the visit; "I did confess, but I confessed a lie" (E23).
- "All black". The execution (E22, E24). "I, not in deed, but in effect, was the true murderer" (E108).
- Belief beat: Victor sat through the whole trial; he heard about the barn.
- Nag: "woken by steps". Someone else was in that barn, or Justine dreamed it.
- Cliffhanger: a glacier, and the suspect's own statement, "and this time I want to look at the ink".

## ep05 — Two Voices (2,300)
- Cold open: the creature, "I too can create desolation" (E32 area).
- Montanvert (E26); the creature's life in outline: the De Laceys, the reading, the fire, the shot (E27–E31).
- The barn passage (E33), side by side with the court (E109). Kessler.
- T1 reveal at the end of act two: Judith and the conservator's count (E92). Walton's own line again (E61).
- Belief beat: the creature's most eloquent words carry the most of Victor's ink.

- Cliffhanger: Victor agrees to make a companion, and goes to the remotest island he can find.

## ep06 — The Window (2,300)
- Cold open: "I shall be with you on your wedding-night." (E38)
- England and Scotland with Henry; Henry at Perth; Victor alone on the island (E36). The find E93.
- The window (E37, E110); the threat as Victor understood it (T2 held). The basket at night (E41).
- Ireland: the depositions, the finger marks, Nugent's boat (E42–E45). The body. The ravings (E111).
- Reber and Okafor on the timeline (E113): found at ten, not cold; Victor still on Orkney; islanders' evidence.
- Belief beat: "I called myself the murderer of William, of Justine, and of Clerval."
- Nag (the theory's wall): Orkney. If Victor, then not alone (S5). Two boats (E45).
- Cliffhanger: home to Geneva. A wedding, and the night he had been warned about.

## ep07 — Evian (2,300)
- Cold open: "Oh! Peace, peace, my love… this night is dreadful, very dreadful."
- Elizabeth's life; her letter (E50); the wedding day on the lake.
- The inn: the pistol; sending her to bed; the passages; the scream, twice (E52, E53) — T2 lands; E85 read.
- The window, the shot, the search; "a form conjured up by my fancy" (E56, E115).
- Kessler and Okafor: the theory at full strength, and what it costs (Victor killing the person he loved).
- Belief beat: the only witness to the figure was Victor.
- Nag: two screams, and nobody can say where he was standing. Open.
- Alphonse dies (E57). Cliffhanger: T3, the admission register (E94): "To be kept apart."
