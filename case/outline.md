# Outline: *Darkness and Distance*, season one

Eight episodes, target 2,300 words each (±15%), about 15 minutes of audio. Beats per episode: cold open → hook →
world before → the event → investigation & aftermath → the unresolved. Evidence IDs from `evidence.md`, rules from
`bible.md`.

## ep00 — Trailer (about 300 words, 2 minutes)
- Cold open: Walton at midnight, "I am interrupted. What do these sounds portend?" (E70).
- Thesis, in the Serial mould: for the last year she has checked a sea captain's letters against the record; the
  more she learns, the stranger it gets, and the more sources back it up.
- Stakes: five dead, a young woman hanged for a murder she didn't commit, the suspect never caught, last seen on
  the ice.
- The hunt: Kent, Geneva, Munich, Paris, Savoy, Lloyd's List. Every record agrees with the letters.
- Mission: Justine's name is still in the register. Can it be cleared?
- The question: could it really be true? "Episode one is out now."

## ep01 — The Man on the Ice (2,300)
- Cold open: Walton's August 5th letter, the giant on the sledge (E02).
- Hook: Judith's email; the sea chest; "five deaths and nobody was ever tried for any of them except a girl who didn't do it".
- World before: who Walton was, the letters to Margaret, no year (E01, rule 3). Judith on Robert.
- Event: the second sledge, the European on the ice (E03, E04).
- Investigation: the three layers, explained simply (rule 7); Judith shows the two inks (E61, E62). The readers introduced.
- Unresolved: Victor's opening words to Walton (E05) and Tessa's line: he says he made the one he was chasing.
- Rules shown: 3, 6, 7, 8 (first mention). Hook: "The first death is a little boy's." (The book gives no age.)

## ep02 — Plainpalais (2,300)
- Cold open: Alphonse's letter, "the print of the murder's finger was on his neck" (E10).
- World before: the Frankenstein household; Caroline's death (E07); Elizabeth; Justine's history (E11); William's eyes.
- Event: May 7th, the walk, the search, five in the morning (E10, E12). The missing miniature.
- Investigation: Okafor on manual strangulation and what a finger mark means (opinion, labelled). Victor's journey,
  the storm, the figure on Salêve (E13), seen only by Victor. His silence and his reasons (E14).
- Unresolved: Ernest's news: "the murderer has been discovered". Justine.
- Rules shown: 5, 11 (first finger mark).

## ep03 — All Black (2,400)
- Cold open: "The ballots had been thrown; they were all black" (E22).
- World before: Justine as a person: twelve years old when she came to the house, the mother, the confessor (E11).
- Event: the trial at eleven: the market-woman, the pocket, the miniature round William's neck (E16–E19).
- Investigation: Reber has found the trial file (E25a), which matches Victor's account; Reber on Genevan procedure, circumstantial evidence and why confessions mattered; Justine's defence
  (E20), "some steps disturbed her" (plant for ep05); Elizabeth's speech (E21); the false confession (E23).
- Aftermath: the execution (E24). The register still names her (E25).
- Unresolved: Victor sat in that court knowing, he says, who did it. "So what had he done?"
- Rules shown: 17; discrepancy 12 planted (pocket).

## ep04 — The Workshop (2,300)
- Cold open: "the dissecting room and the slaughter-house furnished many of my materials" (E09).
- World before: Victor at seventeen, Ingolstadt, Krempe and Waldman (E07, E08).
- Event: two years of work; vaults and charnel houses; the November night (E09, E09a).
- Investigation: Tessa can't test the science and says so; what she can test is the crime: body-snatching. Okafor on
  what an eight-foot body would need, and why he won't go further. Reber on the law about the dead.
  Victor's fever; Clerval nursing him (E09b). The creature disappears for "nearly two years" (E15).
- Unresolved: Victor met him again, on a glacier, and listened for hours. "Next time: the suspect's statement."
- Rules shown: 1, 2, 5.

## ep05 — The Suspect's Statement (2,500)
- Cold open: "I grasped his throat to silence him, and in a moment he lay dead at my feet." (E32) — Creature reader.
- Hook: chain of custody, now critical (rules 7, 8): the voice we hear passed through Victor's hands.
- World before: the hovel, the De Laceys, learning to speak and read; Safie; the copies of letters (E27, E28).
- Events: Felix's stick (E29); the fire (E30); the girl in the river and the gun (E31); Geneva; William (E32); the barn (E33).
- Investigation: the barn matches Justine's testimony: steps (E20 vs E33). Discrepancy: pocket vs folds of her
  dress (rule 12); Judith: the barn passage carries second-ink corrections like the rest of the creature's speech (E62), so it can't
  stand on its own; but the steps are also in the Geneva trial file (E25a), written down in court years before Victor
  told anyone anything.
  The Felix and Safie letters Walton saw (E63), and that they are missing (E64).
- Unresolved: the demand, the promise to go to South America, Victor's "I consent" (E34, E35).
- Rules shown: 7, 8, 10d, 12, 20 (crimes against the creature).

## ep06 — The Irish Shore (2,400)
- Cold open: Kirwin's deposition: "the black mark of fingers on his neck" (E44).
- World before: Henry Clerval as a person (E09b, E36), the trip, Perth (E40).
- Event: Orkney; the female destroyed; "I shall be with you on your wedding-night" (E37, E38, E39); the basket at
  sea (E41); the landing in Ireland; the depositions (E42–E45).
- Investigation: Victor sees the body; his ravings (E47); the grand jury's alibi (E47, E48). Okafor on the pattern
  (rule 11). Reber on grand juries. Two boats (rule 16). The open question of Perth to Ireland (E46, rule 15).
- Unresolved: Victor goes home to marry, with a pistol.
- Rules shown: 10e, 11, 15, 16, 18.

## ep07 — Evian (2,400)
- Cold open: "a shrill and dreadful scream… the scream was repeated" (E53).
- World before: Elizabeth as a person: adopted, Justine's defender, her May letter (E21, E50).
- Event: the wedding, the boat, Evian, the pistol, the scream, the mark, the figure at the window, the lake (E51–E56).
- Investigation: the alternative theory, stated fairly: Victor did it (no alibi, alone saw the figure, earlier
  confessions in fever, laudanum). Tested against the pattern and the alibis (rule 11). Okafor: the mark alone can't
  tell you whose hand. Tessa's honest position: weaker, not impossible, until episode 8.
- Aftermath: Alphonse dies (E57); the cell (E58); the Geneva judge (E59).
- Unresolved: Victor turns hunter.
- Rules shown: 11, 19; discrepancy 14 planted (screams).

## ep08 — Darkness and Distance (2,500)
- Cold open: "lost in darkness and distance" (E72) — hold the line, then back up.
- Event: the chase (E60, E68, E69); the ship; the mutiny (E65); Victor's death (E66, E67).
- Investigation: the cabin, the one clean layer (rule 9, E70, E71): the creature confesses to Clerval without Victor
  in the chain; "strangled the innocent as they slept" vs Elizabeth's scream (rule 14).
- Tally: what is proven, what is likely, what is open (E46, E64, E67, E73). Justine still on the register (E25).
- Ending: Ernest alive (E75); the funeral pile never found (E73). Judith closes the chest. Last words: the book's.
- Rules shown: 9, 10, 14, all paid off.
